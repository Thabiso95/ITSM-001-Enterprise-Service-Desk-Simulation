4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

First Contact Resolution (FCR)

Measures the percentage of incidents resolved during the initial support interaction without requiring further escalation or follow-up.

Formula:

FCR = (Tickets resolved at first contact / Eligible tickets) × 100

Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR)

Measures the average time taken to resolve incidents.

Formula:

MTTR = Total resolution time / Number of resolved incidents

First Response Time

Measures the time between ticket creation and the first response from a technician.

SLA Compliance

Measures the percentage of tickets handled within the agreed service-level targets.

Formula:

SLA Compliance = (Tickets meeting SLA targets / Tickets with applicable SLA targets) × 100

Escalation Rate

Measures the percentage of tickets escalated to higher support levels.

Formula:

Escalation Rate = (Escalated tickets / Total tickets) × 100

7. Operational Improvement Goals

As the ticket register grows, the service desk should aim to:

1. Reduce recurring incidents through knowledge-base documentation.
2. Improve first-contact resolution where appropriate.
3. Maintain accurate ticket priorities.
4. Reduce unnecessary escalations.
5. Escalate security-sensitive incidents promptly.
6. Monitor response and resolution times.
7. Maintain clear communication with users.
